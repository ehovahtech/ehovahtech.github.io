ABOUT EHOVAH

IT support that behaves like infrastructure, not a favour

We built Ehovah Technologies around one idea — devices, servers and domains all fail on the same day if nobody's watching them. So one team watches all of it.

OUR APPROACH

Diagnose first. Quote honestly. Fix it properly.

Most IT problems get worse because they're patched instead of diagnosed. Ehovah Technologies starts every job — a laptop repair or a server migration — with a real diagnostic, so the fix addresses the actual cause, not just the symptom.

That same discipline carries into our AMC plans and backup design: scheduled checks instead of reactive firefighting, and a documented plan you can actually see, not a black box.
